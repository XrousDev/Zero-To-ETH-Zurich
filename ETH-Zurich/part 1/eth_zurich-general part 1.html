Section 1: Problem-Solving Tactics

The ETH Zürich (and Swiss Matura) exams test not formula memorization, but the ability to model systems and think like an engineer.

1. Divide and Conquer

Break a complex problem into independent subsystems.
Physics example: For a cylinder rolling down an inclined plane, write translational kinematics (Newton) and rotational dynamics (torque) as completely separate equations, then link them with $v = \omega r$.

2. Reverse Engineering

Work backward from the desired final result.
Math example: If asked for the area between two curves, reason: "I need a definite integral → I need limits → I need intersection points from $f(x)=g(x)$."

3. Debug Your Own Work

Apply the "edge-case testing" mindset from software.
Math example: When you derive a general formula, plug in $x=0$, $x=1$, or $x \to \infty$ to test whether the result makes sense.

4. Visualization

Geometry and mechanics problems cannot be solved without drawings. Free-body diagrams (FBD) and function graphs dramatically reduce error rates.

5. Dimensional Analysis

Check the consistency of units in your final formula. Physics example: If you derived an energy equation, the unit must be $kg \cdot m^2/s^2$ (Joule).

Section 2: Study Strategies

1. Deep Work

Uninterrupted focus is essential. Use the "Pomodoro" technique (50 min focus, 10 min break) and completely switch off your phone.

2. Active Recall & Spaced Repetition

Reading formulas is passive. Use Anki or Notion to periodically test yourself.

3. Feynman Technique

The best way to understand a topic is to explain it to someone who knows nothing about it.

4. Past Paper Analysis

You must solve ETH's previous "Aufnahmeprüfung" exams. Every wrong answer should be recorded in a dedicated "Error Log".

5. Avoiding Burnout

Mental health is critical in a 5-year process. Take one full day off (Sunday) each week.

Section 3: Oral Exam Strategies

In the Swiss system, the "Mündliche Prüfung" (oral exam) is decisive. You must narrate your thought process.

1. Narrating the Thought Process

Even if you take a wrong turn, if the examiner hears your reasoning, you get points.

  • EN: "First, I will resolve the forces into their components, and then..."
  • DE: "Zuerst werde ich die Kräfte in ihre Komponenten zerlegen, und dann..."

2. Buying Time

  • EN: "Let me quickly sketch this out to visualize the problem better."
  • DE: "Lassen Sie mich das kurz skizzieren..."

3. Self-Correction

  • EN: "Wait, my dimensional analysis fails here. I must have dropped a factor of x."
  • DE: "Moment, meine Einheitenprüfung schlägt fehl..."

Section 7: Question Bank (Part 1 – 40 Questions)

Note: All questions are original and adapted problems in the ETH/Matura style.

A. Algebra, Polynomials, and Functions

Q1 Hard Algebra 12m
EN: Show that $P(x) = x^4 - 6x^3 + 13x^2 - 12x + 4$ is a perfect square and write it in the form $Q(x)^2$.
DE: Zeigen Sie, dass $P(x)$ ein vollständiges Quadrat ist, und schreiben Sie es in der Form $Q(x)^2$.
💡 Strategy: Assume $P(x) = (x^2+ax+b)^2$ and match coefficients.
Solution: $P(x) = (x^2-3x+2)^2 = (x-1)^2(x-2)^2$.
Q2 Easy Algebra 5m
EN: Simplify $\frac{2x}{x^2-1} - \frac{1}{x-1}$.
DE: Vereinfachen Sie $\frac{2x}{x^2-1} - \frac{1}{x-1}$.
Solution: $\frac{1}{x+1}$ ($x \neq 1$).
Q3 Hard Inequality 10m
EN: Solve the inequality $0 < \frac{x+2}{x-1} \le 1$.
DE: Lösen Sie die Ungleichung $0 < \frac{x+2}{x-1} \le 1$.
Solution: $(-\infty, -2)$.
Q4 Medium Inverse Function 7m
EN: Find the inverse of $f(x) = \sinh(x) = \frac{e^x - e^{-x}}{2}$.
DE: Bestimmen Sie die Umkehrfunktion von $f(x) = \sinh(x)$.
Solution: $f^{-1}(x) = \ln(x + \sqrt{x^2+1})$.
Q5 Hard Logarithms 8m
EN: Solve $\log_{10}(x) + \log_{10}(x-0.9) = -1$.
DE: Lösen Sie $\log_{10}(x) + \log_{10}(x-0.9) = -1$.
Solution: $x = 1$ (domain $x > 0.9$).
Q6 Easy Inverse Function 4m
EN: If $f(x) = \frac{2x+3}{x-4}$, find $f^{-1}(5)$.
DE: Wenn $f(x) = \frac{2x+3}{x-4}$, berechnen Sie $f^{-1}(5)$.
Solution: $f^{-1}(5) = \frac{23}{3}$.
Q7 Medium Limits 6m
EN: Evaluate $\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{x - \sin(x)}{x^3}$.
DE: Berechnen Sie $\lim_{x\to 0} \frac{x - \sin(x)}{x^3}$.
Solution: $\frac{1}{6}$ (L'Hôpital or Taylor).
Q8 Hard Optimization 12m
EN: Find the dimensions of the rectangle with maximum area inscribed under the parabola $y = 4 - x^2$ above the x-axis.
DE: Bestimmen Sie die Abmessungen des Rechtecks mit maximalem Flächeninhalt unter der Parabel $y = 4 - x^2$.
Solution: $\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \times \frac{8}{3}$.

B. Calculus: Limits, Derivatives, Integrals, Series

Q9 Medium Parametric 6m
EN: For $x = t^3-3t$, $y = t^2-2t$, find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ at $t=2$.
DE: Für $x = t^3-3t$, $y = t^2-2t$: Berechnen Sie $\frac{dy}{dx}$ bei $t=2$.
Solution: $\frac{2}{9}$.
Q10 Medium Related Rates 7m
EN: A 10 m ladder slides with its base moving at 0.5 m/s. How fast is the top sliding down when the base is 6 m from the wall?
DE: Eine 10 m Leiter: Die Basis gleitet mit 0.5 m/s. Wie schnell gleitet die Spitze, wenn die Basis 6 m entfernt ist?
Solution: $0.375$ m/s downward.
Q11 Hard Volume 9m
EN: Find the volume generated by rotating $y=\sqrt{4-x^2}$ on $[-2,2]$ about the x-axis.
DE: Bestimmen Sie das Volumen des Rotationskörpers von $y=\sqrt{4-x^2}$ auf $[-2,2]$ um die x-Achse.
Solution: $\frac{32\pi}{3}$.
Q12 Medium Improper Integral 7m
EN: Evaluate $\int_0^\infty x e^{-x} dx$.
DE: Berechnen Sie $\int_0^\infty x e^{-x} dx$.
Solution: $1$.
Q13 Hard Maclaurin 9m
EN: Write the first 3 non-zero terms of the Maclaurin series for $\cos(x)$.
DE: Schreiben Sie die ersten 3 nicht-null Terme der Maclaurin-Reihe für $\cos(x)$.
Solution: $1 - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^4}{24}$.
Q14 Medium ODE 7m
EN: Solve the differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx} = 2xy$.
DE: Lösen Sie die Differentialgleichung $\frac{dy}{dx} = 2xy$.
Solution: $y = K e^{x^2}$.
Q15 Medium Differentiability 6m
EN: Find $a$ and $b$ so that $f(x)=\begin{cases}ax^2+2 & x<2\\ x^3-bx & x\ge 2\end{cases}$ is differentiable at $x=2$.
DE: Bestimmen Sie $a, b$, so dass $f$ an $x=2$ differenzierbar ist.
Solution: $a=4.5$, $b=-6$.
Q16 Hard Bayes 8m
EN: A disease affects 1% of a population. A test has 99% sensitivity and 5% false-positive rate. If a person tests positive, what is the probability they actually have the disease?
DE: Eine Krankheit betrifft 1%. Der Test hat 99% Sensitivität und 5% Falsch-Positiv-Rate. Wie groß ist die Wahrscheinlichkeit bei positivem Test?
Solution: $\approx 0.1667$ (16.67%).
Q17 Hard Wedge Dynamics 12m
EN: A frictionless wedge of mass $M$ and angle $\theta$ rests on a horizontal floor. A block of mass $m$ is released on it. Find the horizontal acceleration $a_M$ of the wedge if it is free to slide.
DE: Ein reibungsfreier Keil $M$, Winkel $\theta$, Block $m$. Bestimmen Sie die horizontale Beschleunigung $a_M$.
Solution: $a_M = \frac{mg\sin\theta\cos\theta}{M + m\sin^2\theta}$.
Q18 Medium Series 6m
EN: Find the sum of $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n}{2^n}$.
DE: Bestimmen Sie die Summe von $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{n}{2^n}$.
Solution: $2$.

C. Vectors, 3D Geometry, Linear Algebra

Q19 Easy Cross Product 4m
EN: Find $\vec{u}\times\vec{v}$ for $\vec{u}=(1,2,-1)$, $\vec{v}=(3,0,2)$.
DE: Kreuzprodukt für $\vec{u}=(1,2,-1)$, $\vec{v}=(3,0,2)$.
Solution: $(4,-5,-6)$.
Q20 Medium Determinant 6m
EN: Compute $\det\begin{pmatrix}1&2&-1\\0&3&2\\1&-1&0\end{pmatrix}$.
DE: Berechnen Sie die Determinante.
Solution: $9$.
Q21 Hard Eigenvalues 8m
EN: Find the eigenvalues of $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$.
DE: Bestimmen Sie die Eigenwerte.
Solution: $\lambda \in \{1, 3\}$.
Q22 Medium Line-Plane 7m
EN: Find the intersection of the plane $x+y+z=6$ with the line $\frac{x-1}{2}=\frac{y}{1}=\frac{z+1}{-1}$.
DE: Schnittpunkt von Ebene und Gerade.
Solution: $(7,3,-4)$.
Q23 Easy Subspace 4m
EN: Which of the following is NOT a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$?
DE: Welches ist kein Unterraum von $\mathbb{R}^3$?
A) $x+y+z=0$   B) $2x-3y=0$   C) $x+y+z=1$   D) $x=0,y=0$
Solution: C) $x+y+z=1$.
Q24 Medium Rotation 6m
EN: Write the matrix of the linear transformation in $\mathbb{R}^2$ that rotates vectors counterclockwise by 90°.
DE: Matrix der 90°-Drehung gegen den Uhrzeigersinn in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
Solution: $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$.
Q25 Hard Skew Lines 10m
EN: Find the shortest distance between the skew lines $L_1:(1,0,1)+t(1,2,1)$ and $L_2:(2,1,0)+s(0,1,1)$.
DE: Kürzester Abstand zwischen windschiefen Geraden.
Solution: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$.
Q26 Medium Rank 7m
EN: Find the rank of $\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&6\\1&1&1\end{pmatrix}$.
DE: Bestimmen Sie den Rang der Matrix.
Solution: $2$.

D. Probability and Statistics

Q27 Easy Permutation 4m
EN: How many distinct 10-letter words can be formed from the letters of "MATEMATİK"?
DE: Wie viele verschiedene 10-Buchstaben-Wörter aus "MATEMATİK"?
A) $10!$   B) $\frac{10!}{2!2!2!}$   C) $\frac{10!}{3!2!}$   D) $\frac{10!}{3!}$
Solution: B) $\frac{10!}{2!2!2!}$.
Q28 Medium Conditional 5m
EN: If $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.5$, $P(A\cup B)=0.7$, find $P(A|B)$.
DE: Wenn $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.5$, $P(A\cup B)=0.7$, berechnen Sie $P(A|B)$.
Solution: $0.4$.
Q29 Hard PDF 8m
EN: Given the PDF $f(x)=cx^2$ for $0\le x\le 3$, find $c$ and $E[X]$.
DE: Für $f(x)=cx^2$, $0\le x\le 3$: Bestimmen Sie $c$ und $E[X]$.
Solution: $c=\frac{1}{9}$, $E[X]=2.25$.
Q30 Medium Binomial 6m
EN: A fair coin is tossed 5 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads?
DE: Eine faire Münze wird 5 Mal geworfen. Wahrscheinlichkeit für genau 3 Mal Kopf?
Solution: $\frac{5}{16} = 0.3125$.
Q31 Easy CLT 4m
EN: According to the Central Limit Theorem, the distribution of the sample mean approaches which distribution for large $n$?
DE: Welcher Verteilung nähert sich der Stichprobenmittelwert für großes $n$?
A) Binomial   B) Poisson   C) Normal   D) Exponential
Solution: C) Normal.
Q32 Medium Normal 6m
EN: Given $\mu=50$, $\sigma=5$, find $P(X>60)$.
DE: Mit $\mu=50$, $\sigma=5$: Berechnen Sie $P(X>60)$.
Solution: $0.0228$ (2.28%).

E. Mechanics

Q33 Medium Projectile 7m
EN: A projectile is launched at 20 m/s at 30°. Find the time of flight ($g=10$).
DE: Ein Projektil mit 20 m/s unter 30°. Bestimmen Sie die Flugzeit ($g=10$).
Solution: $2$ s.
Q34 Medium Energy 6m
EN: A 2 kg object is dropped from 10 m. Find its impact velocity.
DE: Ein 2 kg Objekt fällt aus 10 m. Bestimmen Sie die Aufprallgeschwindigkeit.
Solution: $10\sqrt{2}$ m/s $\approx 14.14$ m/s.
Q35 Hard Rotation 9m
EN: A solid cylinder pulley $M=4$ kg, $R=0.5$ m has a 2 kg mass hanging from its string. Find the downward acceleration.
DE: Vollzylinder-Rolle $M=4$ kg, $R=0.5$ m, hängende Masse 2 kg. Bestimmen Sie die Beschleunigung.
Solution: $5$ m/s².
Q36 Medium SHM 7m
EN: A 1 kg mass is attached to a spring with $k=100$ N/m. Find the period $T$.
DE: 1 kg an einer Feder $k=100$ N/m. Bestimmen Sie die Periode $T$.
Solution: $\frac{\pi}{5}$ s $\approx 0.628$ s.
Q37 Easy Collision 4m
EN: In a perfectly inelastic collision, what happens to the colliding objects?
DE: Was passiert bei einem vollkommen unelastischen Stoß?
A) They separate   B) They stick together   C) Energy is conserved   D) Speed doubles
Solution: B) They stick together.
Q38 Medium Torricelli 7m
EN: Water flows from a hole 5 m below the surface. Find the exit velocity.
DE: Wasser fließt aus einem Loch 5 m unter der Oberfläche. Ausflussgeschwindigkeit?
Solution: $10$ m/s.
Q39 Hard Adiabatic 8m
EN: A monatomic ideal gas doubles its volume adiabatically. Find the pressure ratio ($\gamma=5/3$).
DE: Ein einatomiges ideales Gas verdoppelt sein Volumen adiabatisch. Druckverhältnis ($\gamma=5/3$)?
Solution: $2^{-5/3} \approx 0.315$.
Q40 Medium Orbit 7m
EN: Derive the orbital velocity of a satellite at radius $R$ in terms of $GM$.
DE: Leiten Sie die Bahngeschwindigkeit eines Satelliten bei Radius $R$ in $GM$ her.
Solution: $v = \sqrt{GM/R}$.
